Your choice.

Guardian is a service from People Organised. It uses the information you choose to share to help you examine a message or request. You can start with one message. Connecting an account is optional.

When a check includes a clearly labelled UK company registration number, Guardian can look up that number on Companies House. You can also enter a public UK registered business name and choose to look it up. Only the company number or the business name you choose goes to the register. A phone number, email address or domain you add as a known contact is used for comparison within your check.

Guardian keeps an independent UK source directory. Public policy passages, their publication links and review dates can be reused across checks. This shared collection excludes the customer’s message, identity, contacts and case records.

What stays with Wolfgang

Your sign-in identifier, email address, chosen name, saved checks, selected supporting records and replies are held with your Guardian profile. A forwarded or pasted claim remains a claim. It does not automatically become a fact about you.

Financial documents open on your device. When you save a check, Guardian stores the pages or entries you selected as text. The original PDF stays on your device. For selected organisations, starting with HMRC and its debt collectors, Guardian can compare details against independently chosen official reference pages. No customer message is sent to those websites. For signed-in checks, Wolfgang can also ask Meta to search for an organisation’s published practice. The search uses a public organisation name and a standard question. Your original wording, links, account details and records stay out of the search. Guardian reads the cited official page before showing its words. For certain scam patterns Guardian can consult a public guidance page. The guidance lookup sends only the address of that guidance page. When Google Web Risk is enabled for signed-in checks, Guardian sends Google the exact address of at most one eligible public link from your message or selected evidence. It does not send your message, personal profile, documents or mailbox contents to Google. Google compares that address with its threat lists. When VirusTotal is enabled, Guardian may ask it for an existing report on one public link. It sends a SHA-256 fingerprint, which VirusTotal may recognise, rather than the address or message. Guardian skips links with credentials, query strings, fragments or non-standard paths; it does not visit the suspect site or submit links for scanning. When Community mode is enabled, Google Web Risk checks the eligible public link and VirusTotal can supply an additional existing report on that same link within its allowance. Each provider’s finding and time are shown separately, including when a result is unavailable. A warning from one is not cancelled by a result with no warnings from the other. Results describe what was found at the recorded time; a missing threat report is not proof of safety.

New checks in your Guardian account, the records you select and your follow-ups have no automatic expiry. Wolfgang uses relevant earlier records as context in later checks. You can delete individual checks or your Guardian information. Material previously saved with a fixed expiry keeps that original expiry. Historical excerpts already saved in a different check remain with that check until it is deleted or its original expiry is reached.

Deleting saved checks or your Guardian profile does not reset the free trial. A minimal record of used checks, linked to your People Organised account and containing no message content, is retained to maintain your allowance.

Messages sent to Wolfgang

When a messaging channel is available, Wolfgang receives what you send to his shared contact. This does not give him access to your entire WhatsApp, SMS or social message history. Link your sending account once so incoming messages belong to your Guardian account. Linking a channel does not, by itself, enable assessments or automatic replies.

Messages sent before linking are held for up to 24 hours while you sign in. After you link, they can be checked without sending them again. Delivery queues stop exposing message content after 24 hours and clear it during cleanup. Minimal delivery metadata is kept for up to 30 days to prevent duplicates and diagnose failed delivery. Account checks follow the retention terms above. Standalone guest checks expire after 24 hours. The messaging and email platforms also process communications under their own terms.

Connected accounts

Your accounts page shows the services you have linked. Available providers appear when you choose to add a connection. Permissions are requested separately. Optional paid inbox monitoring requires a separate choice for each supported mailbox. Once enabled it reads new inbox previews, looks for specific warning signs, and can compare payment details with earlier emails you selected. It does not read attachments for this feature, send replies, or send these previews to a model. Flagged excerpts are kept for up to seven days. Dismissing an item removes it; stopping monitoring or disconnecting clears the alerts for that mailbox. Disconnecting stops future access. Information already saved remains in Guardian.

Independent-mailbox credentials are held by the separate mailbox connector. Guardian disables its local access before requesting their removal. If the connector cannot confirm removal, Guardian keeps a minimal non-readable cleanup reference for retry and tells you removal is still pending. You can also revoke access or an app password directly with your mail provider.

Asking Wolfgang a public question

For recognised small talk and general questions about Guardian, we send our own short prompt to Meta in Contributor mode. Meta may use that exchange to train its models. Your chat history and personal records are excluded. Personal conversations and checks use Standard mode, which excludes model training. Enter your sign-in code only on the sign-in screen.

When analysis is connected

The message, supporting records and a bounded selection of relevant saved context for your check are sent to the configured analysis provider. Only relevant records belonging to your account are selected. Relationships inferred between records are labelled as interpretations, with their sources, rather than verified facts. Wolfgang’s answer distinguishes observations, uncertainty and possible next steps. We do not use your private records for advertising or to train shared models.

Connecting an account is not a guarantee that every message is being monitored. Each connection explains what is available. Guardian does not initiate payments or independently confirm a person’s identity.

Access to your information

Authorised People Organised platform operators can see service activity. Access to detailed Guardian context requires additional verification and is recorded in an audit log. Other customers and ordinary organisation administrators do not receive access to your private Guardian records.

Your choices

In your account, you can download your Guardian information or delete your profile and saved checks. You can also delete an individual check from its conversation. Step-by-step deletion instructions are available without signing in. Deleting Guardian information does not delete your People Organised account.

Current beta

Sign-in uses a six-digit email code through People Organised. Guardian is linked to the People Organised account verified through that sign-in. Guardian’s service records use the hosted database. Older Guardian accounts are not merged merely because their email addresses match. Email and messaging connections show their availability separately on your accounts page. Your workspace shows whether assessments are available, and each channel shows its own reply status. Connecting an account does not import your entire mailbox or initiate an assessment.

Never share passwords, one-time codes or full card details. If something urgent is happening, contact the organisation through a route you already trust.

Conversation preferences and free previews

Signed-in customers can ask Wolfgang to remember their preferred reply length, formality and humour. Your account shows these preferences and lets you reset them. The public introduction uses a secure first-party cookie, lasting up to 400 days, to limit introductory exchanges; network and site-wide limits also apply. A personal check requires verification. If you continue to sign-in from the introduction, this tab can carry your recent chat and submitted draft for up to ten minutes. The handoff is encrypted and opens only in the tab with its recovery key. After verification, you review the draft before submitting a check. Recent signed-in chat can be restored in this tab for up to 30 minutes.
