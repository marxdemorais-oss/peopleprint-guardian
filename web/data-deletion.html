Delete your
Guardian information.

You can remove your Guardian profile, saved checks and supporting records from your account.

  1. Sign in with your email address.
  2. Open Your account → Privacy & data.
  3. Choose Delete my Guardian information.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Open your account →

What this removes

This removes your Guardian profile, saved checks, supporting records, follow-ups, saved context and channel links. It also removes locally stored connection credentials and cancels pending replies linked to your Guardian account. For an independent-mailbox connection, Guardian requests removal of the credentials held by its separate connector. If removal cannot be confirmed, local access stays disabled and a minimal cleanup reference is kept for retry. The account screen will tell you when this is still pending.

What remains

A minimal record of used free checks remains linked to your People Organised account. It contains no message, evidence or assessment content. Deleting Guardian information does not delete your People Organised account or information held independently by your email or messaging provider.

If you cannot sign in

Email marx@peopleorganised.com with the subject “Guardian data deletion”. Tell us which email address or messaging contact you used. We will need to verify that the information belongs to you before acting. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

If you only want to stop a connection

Open Your accounts & channels. For an email account, open Manage access → Stop. For a messaging channel, choose Manage messaging → Unlink account. This stops future account access or removes that sending-account link. Information already saved stays in Guardian.
