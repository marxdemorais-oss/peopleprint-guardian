  1. Choose the route you already know.

    Contact the person using details saved before this request arrived.

    “Did you ask me to send £900 to the account ending 4821, and what is it for?”

  3. Keep the outcome clear.

    A denial contradicts the request. No reply leaves it unconfirmed. Pause and check further before acting.

What makes Guardian personal?

It considers a request alongside the relevant history you provide: relationships, agreements, bookings, payments and earlier conversations. That can reveal a contradiction that is invisible in a single message. It can also explain an ordinary change.

Do I have to connect my accounts?

No. You can prepare a copied message, selected text record or relevant fact. You choose which records to bring into a check. Open Connections to see which accounts you can link. Each service asks for its own permission.

What comes with my free account?

Verify your email for one personal check. Complete your profile for five cases each calendar month. If you use your starter check this month, it counts as the first of those five. Add evidence or ask a follow-up about the same case without using another check.

Will Wolfgang watch my inbox for me?

With a free account, you choose what to check. We’re preparing a paid plan that will also let you choose a supported inbox for Wolfgang to watch. If he spots a change, such as new bank details on a supplier’s invoice, you’ll find it under Worth your attention. You’ll control each mailbox in Connections. Paid subscriptions are not on sale yet.

Is this only about money?

No. A deceptive request can target account access, identity information, private documents or your trust. Money is one part of a wider picture.

Can Wolfgang confirm a request or stop a payment?

The current beta offers guidance for checking through a previously trusted route. It does not make automatic callbacks, contact other people or control payments. An independent confirmation is evidence about a particular request, not a guarantee that the action is safe.

What if the answer is uncertain?

Keep the outcome explicit: supported by the available records, contradicted by them, or still unconfirmed. No response does not establish a scam. A familiar sender or a request that fits your history does not, by itself, establish that it is genuine.
